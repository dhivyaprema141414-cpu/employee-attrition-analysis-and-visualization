# Employee Attrition Analysis & Visualization

## Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations as it increases recruitment costs, affects productivity, and leads to the loss of experienced employees.

This project analyzes employee attrition using HR analytics data to identify the key factors contributing to employee turnover and provide data-driven recommendations for improving employee retention.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to understand why employees leave the company. High attrition can negatively impact business performance and workforce stability.

This project aims to answer the following questions:

* Which departments experience the highest attrition?
* Which job roles have the highest turnover?
* Does overtime contribute to employee attrition?
* Does salary influence employee retention?
* How does job satisfaction affect attrition?
* Are newer employees more likely to leave?
* What factors should HR focus on to improve retention?

---

## Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition patterns.
* Identify key factors influencing employee turnover.
* Explore relationships between employee demographics, job characteristics, and attrition.
* Generate actionable business insights.
* Support HR decision-making through data visualization.

## Key Findings

* Employee attrition is a significant organizational challenge.
* Certain departments experience higher employee turnover.
* Overtime is associated with increased attrition.
* Employees with lower income levels tend to leave more frequently.
* Job satisfaction has a strong influence on employee retention.
* Attrition is more common among employees in their early years with the company.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Reduce Excessive Overtime

Implement workload management strategies and promote a healthier work-life balance.

### 2. Improve Employee Satisfaction

Conduct employee engagement programs and collect regular feedback.

### 3. Review Compensation Structures

Evaluate salary policies and ensure competitive compensation.

### 4. Focus on High-Risk Departments

Develop department-specific retention initiatives.

### 5. Strengthen Onboarding Programs

Provide mentoring and career development support for newer employees.

### 6. Encourage Data-Driven HR Decisions

Use workforce analytics to monitor attrition trends and improve decision-making.

## Conclusion

This project successfully identified several factors associated with employee attrition, including overtime, salary, job satisfaction, department, and employee experience.

The insights generated from this analysis can help organizations develop effective retention strategies, improve employee satisfaction, and support data-driven HR decision-making.
